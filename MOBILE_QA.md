# Mobile release acceptance

Automated checks cover logic, real model geometry, offline assets, and production DOM event handlers. Physical Android/iPhone GPU, touch feel, thermal behavior, battery, native installation, and actual browser layout are **not certified by the headless environment**.

## Repeatable device procedure

1. Export the current world from Care & display before testing. Use a separate browser profile for destructive test scenarios; keep the exported file outside browser storage.
2. Open the published HTTPS app at phone portrait and landscape sizes, including a notched phone and 320 px width. Mobile must begin in Observe; tapping before opening Build must not plant. Confirm the initial garden fits between the measured panels, the tray scrolls, and Care/inspector/backup panels remain scrollable with readable controls. Rotation/size, scene, and footer controls should be easy to tap without clipped or overlapping labels. Test the expanded and collapsed tray, landscape toolbar, day/night text, native input focus, and browser chrome resizing.
3. Inspect a seedling juniper, moss, flowers, a cactus, a succulent, and driftwood. Watch rapid growth and bonsai trimming in close-up. Rotate between portrait and landscape and change graphics quality; the selected close-up and relative zoom should stay intact. Reset must restore the complete terrarium in the current orientation.
4. Confirm that no animals or Life controls are visible, including after importing a previous world. Check that archived animal records survive export. Place and move plants/decorations; compare preview size, variant, and shape with the resulting specimen. Sculpt a hill and check individual flower/mushroom/clover/grass root contact, glass clearance, soil detail, bark grain, and shore transparency. Check that switching terrain tools hides the mature plant outline.
5. Trim a bonsai three times, Undo/Redo, choose Let regrow, save, reload, and export/import. Verify its ID, position, health, harvest state, and chosen trim survive.
6. Toggle Glass-free view, mature previews, and Calm view. Reload and confirm the choices persist. Compare automatic reduced motion with the device preference. Growth/care clocks must continue in Calm view.
   Enter the clean photo view with a plant or terrain tool selected. The garden should use the full safe viewport; taps and orbit/pinch gestures must not plant or sculpt. Restore the interface and confirm editing works again. On desktop, move the pointer quickly across the soil and out of the canvas; previews must track the latest position without lingering after leaving. With keyboard navigation, Escape must dismiss Care even from a focused select and return focus to its toggle; inspection must return focus to Inspect. Held one-shot shortcuts and unrelated modifier shortcuts must not repeat edits.
7. Create several restore points, edit the garden, then restore an earlier point. Confirm the displaced world is still available and Undo restores the session's prior arrangement. Verify export works from Care and the warning banner.
8. In a test profile, induce a storage failure/conflict. The warning must persist after its toast disappears. Export must work. Retry must clear the banner only when saving succeeds; conflicting saves must not overwrite another tab.
9. With an older installed release open, publish a newer release. Check for updates, choose Later, then reopen it from the persistent Update button. Save & update now must keep the same world and progress. Test save failure, offline checks, a stalled check followed by retry, returning online, and another tab activating the update. First installation must not ask to update. In a separate test profile, disable WebGL or block the engine: only recovery controls should be focusable, Download saved world must match the pre-existing file, and Check for updates must remain usable. Retry with no saved world too.
10. Verify native installation and offline reopening in supported Android and iPhone browsers. Wait for offline readiness before disconnecting.
11. Open Care & display → Shape, size & base. Check all four silhouettes, each growing-area size, and every base finish in portrait, landscape, and night lighting. Cancel must leave the garden untouched. Apply must preserve terrain, plant identities/size/health/progress, and frozen animal records; Undo/Redo, export/import, and reload must retain the chosen design. Test New world's matching choices separately. The soil, water, glass, rims, shadows, and camera should follow the footprint; plants must keep their proportions. From a steep overhead angle, confirm there is no gray rectangular shadow or hard-edged base plane. Trigger a backup failure in a separate profile: Apply must retain the form and original garden.

## Density and performance

`npm run bench:scene -- --fixtures test-artifacts` writes reproducible 50-, 120-, and 220-element worlds and reports CPU-only timings. Import those fixtures in the separate test profile. Record the device, browser, release, viewport, and settings.

For each density, run 1× and 20× time for at least 60 seconds in Auto and Performance, then test Cinematic where practical. Use Care's live FPS display and browser profiling to separate animation/simulation work from GPU work. Record median frame rate, long stutters, responsiveness while orbiting/sculpting, and behavior after returning from the background. Include a ten-minute heat/battery observation for sustained play.

Targets for tuning: prefer a steady 60 FPS where the device has headroom; keep a playable 30 FPS target on constrained phones. These are acceptance goals, not measured guarantees. Change detail/shadow/navigation budgets based on those measurements rather than inferring phone performance from NullEngine results.
