 A LITTLE ROOM TO GROW

Welcome to Verdant.

Shape a landscape, fill it with life, and choose how to play. Self-sustaining stays alive without upkeep. Garden care needs nutrients, water, and tending, even while you are away.

Plant a little possibility

Choose something in your field kit, then tap the soil. Green placement previews show a good spot. R rotates; − and + change size. Planting costs seeds in Garden Journey. Free build has unlimited planting.

Make the land your own

Terrain tools raise and lower soil, add rocks, and carve ponds. Drag a terrain brush to sculpt; right-drag or use Observe to orbit.

Keep a world in balance

Trees cast shade, groundcover holds moisture, and fungi feed nearby soil. Weather moves water downhill. Mist or vent to adjust moisture; use 5× or 20× to see changes sooner.

Grow, harvest, discover

Healthy mature plants glow gold when seeds are ready. Harvest to fund new plantings. Inspect to see local conditions or move an element. Growth starts at Rapid 12×. Lock daylight while the clock keeps running in Care & display. The Field Guide tracks eight chapters and five habitat discoveries. Progress saves on this device. Use Care & display to install the app and play offline after one complete online visit.

Backups & restore.

Up to five dated restore points are kept on this device, plus the previous save and update checkpoint. Export a file to keep a copy outside browser storage.

Restoring keeps a recovery copy of your current saved world. Undo also restores this session's previous world. Browser or device data clearing can remove local backups.
