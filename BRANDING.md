# Verdant brand assets

- `assets/verdant-logo.png`: original transparent square emblem.
- `assets/verdant-social-botanical.jpg`: current 1280 × 640 botanical social preview, 198,152 bytes.
- `assets/verdant-social.jpg`: earlier animal-containing card, retained for history.
- `assets/verdant-social-original.png`: original wide key art.
- `assets/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, and `apple-touch-icon.png`: launcher exports of the emblem.

The terrarium dome, two leaves forming a V, layered earth, and small golden sun connect the logo to the living-world game. Forest green, sage, cream, and gold carry through the social artwork. The illustration is promotional key art, not a gameplay screenshot.

Generated using the built-in OpenAI Image Generation tool. Mechanical exports resize and encode the artwork for app icons and GitHub's social card. No image generation service is called by the game.

## Logo generation brief

Create a standalone transparent square emblem for VERDANT, a mobile-first procedural terrarium-building game. A miniature glass terrarium dome contains two lush leaves forming a subtle V and three terraced earth strata. Refined botanical identity; emerald, sage, pale gold; confident shapes, balanced silhouette, small golden sun. No wordmark, mockup, or surrounding ornaments.

## Social generation brief

Use the logo as the identity reference. Landscape 2:1 repository social card. Deep forest-green background, cream title VERDANT, tagline A little world of your own., and BUILD • GROW • NURTURE. A luminous miniature woodland terrarium contains ferns, moss, flowers, a clear pond, Holland Lop rabbit, tree frog, and bumblebee. Cohesive stylized 3D diorama, natural dawn lighting, generous text-safe margins, no interface or watermark.

## Apply the repository social preview

Open the repository's Settings → General → Social preview → Edit → Upload an image. Upload `assets/verdant-social-botanical.jpg` and save. Website Open Graph/Twitter tags already use the botanical card. This setting is separate from the website's Open Graph metadata.

## Botanical artwork brief

Generated with the built-in OpenAI Image Generation tool and mechanically resized/encoded to 1280 × 640 JPEG. Promotional key art, not a gameplay screenshot. Final path: `assets/verdant-social-botanical.jpg`.

Use case: ads-marketing. GitHub social preview for Verdant. Wide 2:1 composition. Warm ivory background, forest-green editorial typography, the exact title “verdant.”, subtitle “A little world of your own”, descriptor “BOTANICAL TERRARIUM GAME”. A realistic open oval glass terrarium on a thin slate base contains fern fronds, cushion moss, clover, asymmetrical juniper bonsai, layered damp soil, mineral grit, driftwood and a clear pond. Soft window lighting, natural textures, restrained glass highlights. Generous text-safe margins. No animals, people, insects, interface controls, glowing particles, sparkles or watermark.
